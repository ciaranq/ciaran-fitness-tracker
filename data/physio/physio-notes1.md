# Exercise plan for shoulder rehab

Mon, 02 Nov 26

#### Background and Goals

- Previous rotator cuff issues on both shoulders, resolved with ultrasound-guided injection
- Residual stiffness but able to sleep on both sides now
- Main goals: general strength program, return to swimming (freestyle), bushwalking
- Does too much cardio, very little strength work
- History of injuring himself when doing gym exercises, wants a safe program

#### Current Complaints

- Right knee: discomfort at the back, worse first thing in the morning, stiffness after rest, no pain during movement; prior meniscus history on this side
- Left leg: sciatica-like symptoms from base of glute down into knee, random onset, can occur at rest
- Right calf: notably tighter than left, likely contributing to right knee pain
- Leg cramps: occasional, sometimes triggered by certain sitting positions
- Shoulders: mild stiffness only, no acute pain currently

#### Physical Assessment Findings

- Right calf significantly tighter than left, flagged as likely source of right knee symptoms
- Left hamstring tighter than right (approx. 75-80 degrees vs. 80-85)
- Lateral hamstring tendon tender on right, but different to described pain
- Medial gastrocnemius belly sensitive on right
- Glutes: left glute tight, reproduced some familiar discomfort
- Low back: nothing provocative found, though back origin not fully ruled out
- ACL, ligament stress tests, and meniscus tests: all clear

#### Exercise Program

- Legs and glutes:
  - Squats with green resistance band around knees (hip-width, push knees out, don’t go too deep)
  - Lunges, bodyweight to start, can add backpack with weight
  - Crab walk with band just above ankles, soft knees
  - Calf raises: start double-leg, progress to single-leg when comfortable
  - Avoid seated knee extension machine
- Shoulders and arms (all in standing where possible):
  - W row with band (floor anchor or door handle), palm down
  - Internal rotation at 45 degrees (elbow at 90, turn in)
  - External rotation with band
  - Lat pull-down on multi-gym, shoulder-width grip (avoid wide grip)
  - Standing press with band or cable
  - Adduction pull-down with high anchor
- General notes:
  - Default: 3 sets of 10 reps
  - Use yellow band to start; adjust grip position on band to vary resistance
  - Perform most exercises in standing to engage legs and core
  - Stop any exercise that aggravates symptoms; call rather than wait

#### Stretching Plan

- Left hamstring: 2 reps x 20-30 seconds (lying on back, towel around foot, knee straight)
- Right hamstring: 1 rep x 20-30 seconds
- Right calf/Achilles stretch: priority given tightness found on assessment
- Glute stretch (figure-4 style): 2 reps each side x 20-30 seconds
